School Life & Events

Academic milestones, music, sports, community gatherings, and celebrations that shape the STALOS experience.

Music & MDD

The school choir and performing groups take part in Archdiocesan Music, Dance and Drama competitions and regularly support parish liturgies.

Games & Sports

Students compete and train in a range of games and sports as part of a balanced school programme.

PTA & Community

The Parents and Teachers Association organises meetings, fundraising (including the annual marathon), and joint projects for school development.

Annual highlights

Academic calendar

Term openings, continuous assessment, internal examinations, and preparation for UNEB exams form the backbone of the year. Parents are kept informed through meetings and official communications.

Founders’ Day reflections, prize-giving, cultural days, religious celebrations, and community fundraising events bring the whole STALOS family together.
