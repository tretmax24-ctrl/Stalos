Admissions

We welcome new students into day and boarding sections at O-Level and A-Level.

Who we admit

STALOS is a mixed secondary school. We admit boys and girls into day and boarding programmes across ordinary and advanced levels, subject to capacity and requirements.

How to apply

Contact the school office for the current process, entry requirements, and vacancies. Visits are encouraged so families can see the campus and speak with staff.

Fees overview

Figures from the 2025 school magazine. Confirm current rates with the bursar’s office.

O-Level

  • Day tuition: UGX 280,000
  • Boarding tuition: UGX 550,000

A-Level

  • Day tuition: UGX 300,000
  • Boarding tuition: UGX 650,000

Additional functional fees, registration, uniform, and other requirements apply. Full details from the school bursar.

What you can expect

Academic focus

Clear teaching, continuous assessment, and preparation for national examinations.

Boarding life

Structured environment with improved water supply, renovated facilities, and pastoral care.

Holistic formation

Music, vocational skills, clubs, sports, and values education alongside academics.
