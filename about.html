About STALOS

A mixed day and boarding school rooted in Catholic principles, serving Nabbingo since 1986.

Our story

Opened 2 February 1986 as Pere Bec Secondary School. Renamed St. Aloysius Senior Secondary School on 5 November 2009. Today led by Head Teacher Mr. Matte Paul Remmy Kizito.

Who we are

Catholic-founded, open to all. Holistic education for 549 students (nearly half boarding) and 42 teachers.

Vision

Quality and affordable education in the Archdiocese of Kampala.

Motto

Knowledge is our strength

Foundation

Catholic identity, inclusive spirit, responsible citizens.

Leadership

Head Teacher

Mr. Matte Paul Remmy Kizito — since 2017.

Governance

Board Chair Mr. Jude Musisi; Parish Priest Rev. Fr. Charles Kiggundu; active PTA.
