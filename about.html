About STALOS

A mixed day and boarding school rooted in Catholic principles, serving Nabbingo and the wider community since 1986.

Our Story

St. Aloysius Senior Secondary School began through the tireless efforts of Fr. Pere Bec. Land was acquired and early structures built. On 2 February 1986 the school officially opened as Pere Bec Secondary School.

On 5 November 2009, under the guidance of the parish, it was renamed St. Aloysius Senior Secondary School in honour of the Jesuit saint. Today the school continues to grow under the leadership of Head Teacher Mr. Matte Paul Remmy Kizito.

Who We Are

We are a Catholic-founded institution that remains open and welcoming to students of all backgrounds. Our focus is holistic education — academic excellence, moral formation, practical skills, and faith.

Nearly half of our 549 students are boarders. They are supported by 42 teachers and a dedicated non-teaching team.

Vision

To be a fast growing institution providing quality and affordable education in the Archdiocese of Kampala.

Motto

Knowledge is our strength

Foundation

Catholic identity, inclusive spirit, and a commitment to forming responsible citizens.

Leadership

Head Teacher

Mr. Matte Paul Remmy Kizito
Serving since 2017. Under his leadership the school has seen steady growth in enrolment, infrastructure improvements, and a stronger academic and co-curricular culture.

Governance

The school is guided by a Board of Governors chaired by Mr. Jude Musisi, with close pastoral support from the Parish Priest, Rev. Fr. Charles Kiggundu, and an active Parents and Teachers Association.
