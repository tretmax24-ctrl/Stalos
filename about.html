About STALOS

A mixed day and boarding school rooted in Catholic principles, serving Nabbingo since 1986.

Our story

St. Aloysius Senior Secondary School began through the efforts of Fr. Pere Bec. On 2 February 1986 the school opened as Pere Bec Secondary School. On 5 November 2009 it was renamed St. Aloysius Senior Secondary School. Today it continues to grow under Head Teacher Mr. Matte Paul Remmy Kizito.

Who we are

We are a Catholic-founded institution open to students of all backgrounds. Our focus is holistic education — academic excellence, moral formation, practical skills, and faith. Nearly half of our 549 students are boarders, supported by 42 teachers.

Vision

To be a fast growing institution providing quality and affordable education in the Archdiocese of Kampala.
