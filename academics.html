Academics

Strong classroom teaching combined with practical skills and character formation.

O-Level & A-Level

We offer the full secondary curriculum at both ordinary and advanced levels. Teaching is supported by continuous assessment, seminars, and a growing resource base that includes computers, Wi-Fi, and an updated library.

Holistic approach

Academic work sits alongside music, sports, clubs, and vocational training. Our goal is to form young people who are academically capable, morally grounded, and ready for further study or work.

Vocational & skills programmes

Hands-on training that gives students practical options beyond the classroom.

Tailoring & Hairdressing

Practical skills with real market value.

Plumbing & Electronics

Technical training for self-reliance and employment.

Other trades

Shoe making, liquid soap production, catering, book making and more.

Beyond the classroom

Music & Choir

STALOS has a strong reputation for music. The school choir regularly takes part in Archdiocesan competitions and supports parish liturgies.

Clubs & Activities

Students can join debating, mathematics, Interact, TASO, Nkobazambogo and other clubs that develop leadership, communication and service.
